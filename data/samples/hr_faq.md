# Onboarding FAQ

## Q: When will I receive my laptop and access card?
You collect your laptop and access card from the IT Helpdesk on Day 1. If you join remotely, the laptop is couriered to your address before your joining date.

## Q: Who do I contact if my email or MFA setup fails?
Contact the IT Helpdesk at helpdesk@northwind.example or dial extension 4000. MFA setup requires the laptop to be collected first.

## Q: When is my first salary credited?
Salary is credited on the last working day of each month. Bank details and the tax declaration form must be submitted to Payroll by Day 2 to be included in the first cycle.

## Q: Can I work from home in my first weeks?
Work from home is allowed up to 2 days per week after you complete your first 10 working days, and needs manager approval.

## Q: How do I claim expenses?
Submit the claim in the expense portal within 30 days of the spend and attach receipts.

## Q: What is the dress code?
Business casual on office days. Client-facing meetings may require formal wear as advised by your manager.
