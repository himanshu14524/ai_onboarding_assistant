# Northwind Technologies - Employee Onboarding Policy

## Day 1 Joining Formalities
- Mandatory: Sign the offer acceptance and NDA. Owner: HR. Deadline: Day 1.
- Mandatory: Collect laptop and access card. Owner: IT Helpdesk. Deadline: Day 1.
- Mandatory: Set up corporate email and MFA. Owner: IT Helpdesk. Deadline: Day 1. Depends on: Collect laptop and access card.
- Recommended: Meet your buddy and manager for the team introduction. Owner: Reporting Manager. Deadline: Day 1.

## Payroll and HR Systems
- Mandatory: Submit bank details and tax declaration form. Owner: Payroll. Deadline: Day 2. Depends on: Sign the offer acceptance and NDA.
- Mandatory: Set up timesheet and leave portal profile. Owner: HR. Deadline: Day 2. Depends on: Set up corporate email and MFA.

## Compliance Training
- Mandatory: Complete Information Security Awareness training. Owner: Compliance Team. Deadline: Day 3. Depends on: Set up corporate email and MFA.
- Mandatory: Complete Code of Conduct and POSH training. Owner: Compliance Team. Deadline: Day 3. Depends on: Set up corporate email and MFA.

## Working Hours and Leave
Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday, with a flexible window of one hour either side.
New joiners are eligible for 1 casual leave per month during the first six months. Sick leave requires a medical certificate for absences longer than 2 days.
Employees can work from home up to 2 days per week after completing the first 10 working days, subject to manager approval.

## Expenses and Assets
Expense claims must be submitted in the expense portal within 30 days of the spend, with receipts attached.
A lost or stolen laptop or access card must be reported to the IT Helpdesk within 24 hours.
Probation period is 6 months; a confirmation review happens in the final month of probation.

## Security Rules
Never share passwords or MFA codes. Lock your screen when away from your desk. Report suspicious emails to the security mailbox using the Report Phishing button.
